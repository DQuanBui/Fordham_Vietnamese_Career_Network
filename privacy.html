Privacy

How FVCN handles your information

FVCN is a student-run project. We collect only what we need to connect you with a mentor, and we never sell or share it for marketing.

What we collect

  • Coffee chat requests: your name, Fordham email, class year, major or interest, preferred times, message, and an optional resume or LinkedIn link.
  • Event RSVPs: your name and email.
  • "Get involved" forms: your name, email, and what you tell us.
  • Feedback after a chat: your rating and comment. We only quote you on the site if you check the box allowing it, and only with your first name.

Who can see it

  • The mentor you request (or are matched with) sees your request so they can reply.
  • A small group of FVCN organizers can see all requests to match students and keep things running.
  • Nobody else. Access is enforced by the database itself, not just by the website.

Stored on your device

Your browser remembers your name and email (to prefill forms), your roadmap checklist, and private links to your own requests and RSVPs. You can clear this any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

How long we keep it

We keep requests for up to one academic year so mentors and organizers can follow up, then delete them.

Your choices

You can cancel a request from its private status link. To have your information deleted, ask any FVCN organizer, or send a note through the "Get involved" form on the home page.

Data is stored with Supabase (hosted PostgreSQL). Last updated September 2026.
