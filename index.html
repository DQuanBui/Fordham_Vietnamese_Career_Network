    How it works

    From "I have no idea" to a plan in four steps.

    FVCN is for students who don't know where to start. It's especially useful for freshmen and sophomores who haven't met anyone in their field yet.

    1. 01

      Explore a path

      See the roles, skills, and recruiting timeline for each major.

    2. 02

      Find a mentor

      Browse upperclassmen and alumni by major, company, and what they can help with.

    3. 03

      Request a chat

      Pick two times that work and tell them what you'd like to talk about.

    4. 04

      Meet and follow up

      Your mentor confirms a time. Use our playbook to prepare and follow up.

    Beyond careers

    A community, not just a network.

    The best advice often comes from people you actually know. We make time for food, sports, and exploring the city together.

    Soccer Sundays

    Casual weekend games to reset and meet people outside class.

    Basketball runs

    Pickup games and NBA watch parties after a long week.

    Vietnamese food nights

    Phở, bánh mì runs, and home-cooked meals around NYC.

    Casual coffee chats

    Not every conversation has to be formal. Just meet upperclassmen for coffee.

    Study groups

    Finals prep, coding sessions, and finance technicals practice.

    NYC exploration

    Museums, company visits, career fairs, and new neighborhoods together.
